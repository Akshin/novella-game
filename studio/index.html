About the studio

We make games where the story comes first

Throne Studios is an independent mobile game studio. We make interactive stories: games where the player drives the plot and every decision has consequences.

Who we are

Made by people who live and breathe games

Throne Studios was founded by people deeply immersed in games, both as players and as makers. We know what makes a story impossible to put down, and we build every game around two things.

  • I

    Modern technology

    Our own story engine, living animated scenes and a fast release pipeline. Technology lets us tell stories in ways that weren't possible before, and ship new chapters without making players wait.

    • Own story engine
    • Living scenes
    • Chapters without app updates
  • II

    Deep, well-crafted stories

    Characters with a past, worlds with their own rules, and choices with real consequences. We write stories that stay with you long after the last chapter.

    • Characters with depth
    • Thought-out worlds
    • Choices that matter

What we believe

Three studio rules

  • Story first

    If a scene doesn't move the plot forward or reveal a character, it isn't in the game.

  • Choices carry weight

    The player's decisions aren't decoration. The game remembers them and comes back to them in later chapters.

  • Atmosphere in the details

    Art, music and animation all work toward one thing: making you want to come back to the story.
