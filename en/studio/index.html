About the studio

We make games where the story comes first

Throne Studios is an independent mobile game studio. We make interactive stories: games where the player drives the plot and every decision has consequences.

How it started

Two friends and one idea

The studio was founded in early 2026 by two friends who are deeply into games. Akshin has been making games for years, and by the time the studio started, Eva had already written several stories for games. Together we set out to make what we want to play ourselves.

  • A

    Akshin

    Co-founder · engineering

    Senior game developer. Responsible for the technology behind StoryVeil: the story engine, the app, and everything that makes the scenes come alive.

  • E

    Eva

    Co-founder · stories

    Game story writer, with several game stories written before the studio began. Now responsible for the plots and characters of StoryVeil.

What we believe

Three studio rules

  • Story first

    Art, music and interface serve the plot. If a scene doesn't move the story forward, it isn't in the game.

  • Choices carry weight

    The player's decisions aren't decoration. The game remembers them and comes back to them in later chapters.

  • Our own technology

    We built our own story engine. It lets new chapters ship quickly, without an app update.
