How it works

Every choice leaves a mark

StoryVeil is a book where you are the main character. Stories come out in chapters, and the game remembers everything you said and did.

  • Your decisions

    What you say and do changes the plot. The game remembers your choices and comes back to them in later chapters.

  • Relationships

    Every character reacts to your words in their own way. Trust, friendship or rivalry — it's up to you.

  • Special choices

    Some lines and scenes are unlocked with gems. They show the characters from an unexpected side.

  • Living scenes

    Animated backgrounds, characters with emotions, and text that types out like a real conversation.

  • New chapters

    Stories come out in chapters, and new ones arrive regularly. You won't wait long for what happens next.

  • Progress goes with you

    Pick up from any line, even on another device: your saves are stored in the cloud.

First story

Interference

2046. CERN switches on ASTERION, the largest scientific instrument in Europe. On its very first shift, the collider receives data from experiments that haven't happened yet, and footage of people who are already gone.

Mira Koval has been working toward this launch her whole life, ever since the evening her grandfather showed her Jupiter through a telescope. Now she has to work out what exactly she is seeing, and decide whom she can trust.
