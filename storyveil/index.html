How it works

Every choice leaves a mark

A visual novel is an illustrated story you play: you read scenes with the characters and decide what happens next. StoryVeil holds many of these stories in one app — you open it like a library and pick a book.

  • Manystories in one appDifferent genres: sci-fi, romance, post-apocalypse, mystic horror
  • 1–3books per storyA story is a series, like a book saga
  • ~9chapters per bookNew chapters come out every month
  • 7–15endings per storyWhich one you reach depends on your choices

  • Elias lingers at your console.

    Tap a choice

    Your decisions

    What to say, what to do, whom to trust. Choices also shape who your heroine is: at key moments she acts on her own, in line with her stronger trait. The plot branches to one of 7–15 endings; replay to find the others.

  • Tap a character

    Romance and relationships

    Build relationships with up to four characters at once, or stay loyal to one. Each reacts in their own way to the same words, and closer bonds unlock new scenes, routes and endings.

    Special choices

    Premium choices, special romance scenes and routes are unlocked with gems, the game's one currency. The game itself is free to play.

First story

Interference

2046. CERN switches on ASTERION, the largest scientific instrument in Europe. On its very first shift, the collider receives data from experiments that haven't happened yet, and footage of people who are already gone.

Mira Koval has been working toward this launch her whole life, ever since the evening her grandfather showed her Jupiter through a telescope. Now she has to work out what exactly she is seeing, and decide whom she can trust.
