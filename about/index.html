About us

Two founders, one story studio

Throne Studios is an independent studio of two founders building StoryVeil — a visual novel app: a library of interactive stories with animated characters, where your choices decide how each story ends.

We split the work the way a film is made. Eva writes the stories and designs how every scene and character looks. Agshin turns that into code, art and animation. Together we built our own engine and a fully playable first chapter in three weeks.

Agshin Ragimov

Co-founder · Tech · Visuals · Engine

Agshin is a senior full-stack developer who builds everything the player touches: the engine, the build and the visuals.

Engineering
9+ years in full-stack engineering and custom software architecture.
The StoryVeil engine
Built from scratch in Flutter: branching chapters, choices and paid choices, relationship scores and character stats. Playable builds for iOS and Android, plus the browser demo.
Art and animation
Produces the characters, emotion sets and animated backgrounds in our own pipeline, from Eva’s designs.
Story pipeline
A system for creating stories quickly and at high quality: scripts are written in Ink and run on our own Ink runtime; every chapter is automatically checked, built and published to the server, so new chapters reach players without an app update.

Where we are

We have gone as far as two people and our own money can take us

Everything below was built by the two of us, on our own time and our own savings:

  • The game

    Our own engine; chapter 1 of Interference playable end to end, all branches working; choices, paid choices and relationship scores live in the build.

  • The look

    Full cast with emotion sets, animated backgrounds, key art, app icon and a trailer.

  • The launch kit

    Playable iOS and Android builds, a browser demo and our website, two stories ready for launch, a pitch deck and a go-to-market plan.

We are now at the point where every next step needs investment: chapters at the pace players expect (2–3 a month per story), the launch campaign, and the people who make both possible. Without funding we can keep building slowly; with it we launch about 8 weeks after signing.
